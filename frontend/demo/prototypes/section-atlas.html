View the grouping and layout model

The shape slider moves the smallest sections, keeping their cards together. At the reading end, alternate rows reverse direction within each larger subsection. The larger subsection containers follow the same nested arrangement. This is a nested reading snake, not one flattened row sequence across section borders.

At the dependency end, section-level relationships determine layers. A section graph can contain a cycle even if the individual object graph is acyclic, so the layout groups strongly connected sections for ranking. Arrows retain their original endpoints and directions.

Measured section boxes are packed before their larger container is measured. A fixed separation pass prevents container overlaps during blending. That correction can make a group shift as you drag the slider. It does not animate continuously in the background.

“+N outside view” counts omitted positions in grimoire order. It does not mean unavailable source material. Select a marker to read its endpoints; it never expands the omitted interval. Continue still advances one object.

All 36 objects are invented. The render limit remains 18 objects and 48 arrows. The Either/Both choice and numeric limits remain proposals.
