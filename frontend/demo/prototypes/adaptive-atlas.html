View the layout model and current assumptions

The whole grimoire is eligible. Book closeness is the difference in object positions; dependency closeness is shortest-path distance in either direction. This sample assumes authored dependencies point forward in the book. The view contains at most 18 objects and 48 relationships.

  • Dependency ranks are calculated from predecessors, with a nondecreasing rank in book order. Four fixed barycenter passes arrange peers. This keeps both kinds of progress generally downward in this forward-only sample.
  • The shape slider blends that arrangement with a vertical ordered line. A collision pass separates cards before arrows are routed. Sections never supply node coordinates.
  • Every arrow has its own ports. A bounded orthogonal router avoids cards and already occupied line segments. Small gaps at crossings distinguish passing lines from connections. Select an arrow to read its exact meaning.
  • Large boundaries belong to the first section below the whole-book root. Deeper subsections appear in the reader breadcrumb. Headings occupy a reserved gutter outside every arrow route.
  • Only truly adjacent visible objects receive a gold arrow. Continue advances one object even when the destination is outside the current window. There is no bulk expansion.

These are prototype assumptions, including the 18-object limit. Backward dependencies and cycles need their own presentation decision; this sample does not pretend that both orders can always descend together.
